CopilotJ is a conversational multi-agent system for bioimage analysis. Users describe a task in natural language, and a team of agents (leader, kernel, research and enhancement) plans the analysis and runs it in ImageJ through the CopilotJ Bridge plug-in. A pipeline validated on one image can be saved as a reusable workflow and applied to a whole folder in batch.
